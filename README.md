# Product-Sales-Pricing-Scenario-Analysis
## Project objective
An interactive Power BI analytics solution designed to evaluate how product discounting and changes in customer demand can impact sales, profitability, and profit margins.  The dashboard enables business users to simulate different pricing scenarios and compare their potential financial impact against the baseline.

## Business Problem
- Retail businesses frequently use discounts and promotional pricing to increase product demand and drive sales. However, higher discounts can also reduce revenue      per unit and negatively impact profitability and profit margins.
- Business stakeholders therefore need a way to evaluate the potential financial impact of pricing and promotional strategies before making decisions.
- The challenge is to determine whether the potential increase in demand from a discount is sufficient to offset the reduction in selling price.
- This project addresses this challenge through an interactive what-if scenario analysis dashboard.
  
## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to develop an interactive analytics solution that enables business users to:
- Analyze product sales and profitability.
- Evaluate the impact of discounting selected products.
- Simulate potential changes in customer demand.
- Compare scenario-based profits against the baseline forecast.
- Measure the impact of pricing scenarios on profit margins.
- Identify products and geographic areas suitable for targeted
  promotional strategies.
- Support data-driven pricing and promotional decisions.

## Business Questions & Key KPI's
The analysis is designed to answer the following business questions:
1. What is the expected profit under the selected pricing scenario?
2. How does the scenario compare with the original forecast?
3. How does discounting selected products affect profitability?
4. Can an increase in demand offset the impact of lower selling prices?
5. How does the scenario affect overall profit margins?
6. Which products should be considered for promotional strategies?
7. How does the impact vary across different states?
8. What is the cumulative financial impact of the selected scenario?
